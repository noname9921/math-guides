🔷 Exponents
Definition: Exponents tell how many times a number (the base) is multiplied by itself. \[ a^n = \underbrace{a \cdot a \cdot\ a \cdots \ a}_{n \text{ times}} \]
Rules: \[a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}\] \[\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}\] \[\left(a^m\right)^n = a^{mn}\] \[(ab)^n = a^n\cdot\ b^n\] \begin{align*} \left(\frac{a}{b}\right)^n = \frac{a^n}{b^n}\\ a^0 = 1 \quad (a \neq 0)\\ a^1 = a\\ a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}\\ a^{1/n} = \sqrt[n]{a}\\ a^{m/n} = \left(\sqrt[n]{a}\right)^m \end{align*} Exponential Growth/Decay: \begin{align*} A = A_0 (1 + r)^t\\ A = A_0 (1 - r)^t \end{align*} Mistakes to Avoid: \begin{align*} a^m + a^n \neq a^{m+n}\\ (a + b)^n \neq a^n + b^n \end{align*}
